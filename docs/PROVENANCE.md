# Content and Asset Provenance

This record helps contributors and redistributors understand the origin,
licensing, and review status of non-code material in MyanTyper.

## Curriculum

The curriculum text in the per-track modules under `src/lib/curriculum/` was
authored for MyanTyper. Its skill sequence is based on KBDMYAN finger reach,
character frequency, Shift difficulty, visual input order, and Myanmar
orthographic complexity. Contributions follow the content policy in
`docs/CURRICULUM.md`; material from elsewhere includes its source and licence
information.

The project maintainer reviewed the current Myanmar drills for spelling,
spacing, punctuation, keyboard coverage, and typing suitability. This review was
confirmed on 2026-07-16. New lesson material follows the review policy in
`docs/CURRICULUM.md`.

The one-output key-isolation and alternation lines are mechanical keyboard
drills rather than lexical examples. They are included in the recorded
maintainer review above.

Curriculum and documentation created for MyanTyper are distributed under
`LICENSE-CONTENT.md` (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Project artwork

The MyanTyper logo is the project's visual identity. The project icon uses the
same artwork. Public artwork files are stored in `public/`.

Project artwork is distributed under `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` (CC BY-SA 4.0), to
the extent the project holds licensable rights in those assets.

## Fonts

Files in `public/fonts/` are third-party works. They are not covered by the
project's AGPL or CC BY-SA grants. Their individual licences and source links are
recorded in `public/fonts/LICENSES.md` and travel with the public snapshot.

## Maintenance

This record changes alongside curriculum authorship or review status, project
artwork, and bundled third-party material. Externally sourced content is
identified with its author, source, and compatible licence rather than listed
as project-authored material.
